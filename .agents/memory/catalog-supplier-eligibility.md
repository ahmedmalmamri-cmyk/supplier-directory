---
name: Catalog supplier eligibility
description: Explicit decision on how subtype approvals affect existing supplier visibility and access.
---

Require an active, approved subtype offer before any supplier is discoverable or contactable, including suppliers who were visible before this feature. Keep the separate admin activation flag and supplier login intact so suppliers can add the missing declarations.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose immediate application to all suppliers, knowing some current profiles would disappear. Turning off their account access would prevent those suppliers from completing their catalog and confuse editorial moderation with catalog completeness.

**How to apply:** Gate all public results, detail views, counts, and buyer contact actions on approved active offers as well as existing admin status; pending/rejected/removed offers never qualify. Keep protected supplier editing available to active accounts and explain hidden status in their workspace.
