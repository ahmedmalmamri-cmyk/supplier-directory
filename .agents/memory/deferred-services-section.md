---
name: Deferred services section
description: Product-first visibility policy for the services and consultations taxonomy branch.
---

Hide «الخدمات والاستشارات» and its descendants from public product discovery and new registration choices, but retain their records, historical associations, and admin access. Product subgroups, including cake supplies, must remain visible.

**Why:** The owner wants the directory to focus on products for now and explicitly allowed hiding instead of deletion. Hiding is reversible and avoids losing supplier history or suppressing product classifications.

**How to apply:** Treat services as a possible future development phase rather than a paid entitlement until a separate decision defines that offering. Do not purge records or globally deactivate historical supplier associations merely to simplify public browsing. If the services root is renamed, review its visibility rule before release.
