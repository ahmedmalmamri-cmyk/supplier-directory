---
name: Taxonomy icon scope
description: Product taxonomy icon policy for browsing, registration, and administration.
---

Individual item classifications must be text-only; keep icons for sections and groups. Do not add emoji or icon pickers back to individual items in public browsing, search, supplier registration, or the admin category editor.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to remove all icons attached to items and rely on sections/classifications instead. Keeping group icons preserves the distinction between navigating a section and choosing a specific item.

**How to apply:** For any new taxonomy view or editor, treat an item as a named classification under a group rather than a visual tile that needs its own icon. Preserve existing category identities, routes, and supplier links.
