---
name: Gold accent contrast
description: Color contrast rule for the bakery directory's brown and honey-gold identity.
---

Use dark chocolate text on filled honey-gold controls and white text on milk-chocolate primary controls, even when a general instruction says all buttons should have white labels.

**Why:** White on the chosen honey gold has insufficient contrast for small button text (about 2.1:1), while dark chocolate on gold is about 6.6:1. White on the chosen milk-chocolate brown is about 4.6:1.

**How to apply:** When adding accent-colored buttons, badges, or highlighted controls, pair their foreground with the appropriate semantic foreground token rather than hardcoding white. Keep both theme variants legible.
