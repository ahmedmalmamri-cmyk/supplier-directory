---
name: Exact-item inquiries
description: Distinguishes walk-in availability questions from wholesale supply requests.
---

For an in-store purchase, the owner may ask about a precise brand or product type under a generic catalog item without specifying quantity. A generic item association identifies candidate suppliers but never asserts that the requested brand is in stock. Only a supplier's explicit reply confirms availability, a price, and the outlet; alternatives require the buyer's permission.

**Why:** The owner described wanting to find a particular animal-ghee brand at a supplier's sales outlet without placing a bulk order, and emphasized that most suppliers list only the generic item. Treating generic membership as exact stock would mislead buyers.

**How to apply:** Keep this inquiry path distinct from wholesale supply requests. Route it to relevant suppliers, show replies privately, and reveal buyer contact information only through the authenticated chosen-supplier contact action.
