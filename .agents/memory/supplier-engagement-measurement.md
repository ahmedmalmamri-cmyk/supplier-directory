---
name: Supplier engagement measurement
description: The adopted measurement and pricing basis for supplier analytics.
---

Measure the directory's value using qualified contact opportunities, not claimed WhatsApp conversations or sales. A qualified opportunity is one active authenticated buyer contacting the same supplier once within a rolling 30-day window; repeated contacts in that window should not create extra billable opportunities.

**Why:** The app can create and log the handoff to WhatsApp, but it cannot reliably observe message delivery, replies, or completed sales inside WhatsApp.

**How to apply:** Show suppliers and admins page views, qualified contacts, unique buyers, and conversion rate with a clear label that these are directory-created opportunities. Delay per-lead charging until real volume and supplier feedback justify it; prefer transparent subscriptions first.
